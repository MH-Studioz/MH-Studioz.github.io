        HOW TO PLAY

        01 · THE LOOP

        Each round serves seven prompts on a 12, 20 or 30 second clock depending on difficulty. Type any real place that fits the prompt and press ENTER. Answers are typo-tolerant and alias-aware, so “Bombay” and “Mumbai” both land. Wrong guesses shake and cost you time, never a turn.

        02 · RARITY PAYS

        Depth comes from an inverse-log scale over the metric the prompt cares about: population, land area, river length or summit elevation. A megacity scrapes 50–200 m. A one-horse town, a forgotten river or a cold little lake can pay up to 1000 m. The percentile shown on reveal is computed against every valid answer for that prompt, not the whole planet.

        COMMON · 50–200m SOLID · 350m DEEP GEM · 700–1000m

        03 · TOURIST TRAPS

        Vatican City, Everest, the Nile, Tokyo — the answers everyone reaches for. They still count, but the engine flags ⚠ TOURIST TRAP and caps the answer at 150 m. The deepest cuts are almost never the famous ones.

        04 · MODES & DIFFICULTY

        • DAILY 7One deterministic set of seven prompts per date. Same planet, same puzzles.
        • CONTINENT BLITZFive fast prompts locked to a region you pick.
        • HARDCOREUnlabelled tiles. No names, no borders, just your memory.
        • ENDLESSOne shared clock. Every hit banks +5 seconds. Miss once and the drill stops.
        • PINPOINTThe map dives to a mystery coordinate. Name it, or any place within 300 km.

        05 · READING THE MAP

        Cyan pins are your answers. Gold stars are the rarest valid answers you left on the table. The gauge on the right is a logarithmic Earth cross-section — the crust is only the first tenth of the journey, which is exactly why depth is measured in metres.

        06 · STREAKS & THE DAILY CLOCK

        The daily streak counts consecutive local calendar days with a finished Daily 7. Finish one today and one tomorrow and it reads 2; skip a day and it drops back to 1. Extra runs on the same day do not inflate it. A new descent unlocks at local midnight, when the home panel countdown reaches zero — the seed is drawn from the date, so everyone in the world gets the same seven prompts for that day.

        07 · PARTY & CLUBS

        Host a private party and share the six-digit code: one friend makes it a 1v1, more make it a room. Everyone dives the same seven seeded prompts on the host's difficulty, and the strip under the HUD carries the live scoreboard. A club keeps a leaderboard of every diver's deepest descent; boards swap browser-to-browser whenever clubmates party together, so there is no account and no server holding your scores.
