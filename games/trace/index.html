        HOW TO PLAY
        
    READ THE LINE
    
        
    
          You get one circuit centerline with every label stripped off. Type the circuit name and
          press Enter. Aliases count: Monza, Autodromo Nazionale Monza, Temple of Speed — all the same
          answer.
        
    
        
      
          
    • Championship — eight circuits from one title, fresh order every run. MIXED draws from all seven series, FEEDER from F2/F3/F4 and AMERICAN from NASCAR/IndyCar.
      • 
          
    • Podium — pick a season and a Grand Prix to see who finished first, second and third.
      • 
          
    • Daily — the same eight circuits for everyone until local midnight.
      • 
          
    • Party — a host picks the championship, everyone races the same eight.
      • 
          
    • Typing — a shortlist appears as you type. ↑ ↓ highlights one, Enter takes it and guesses; Tab still moves on to the next control.
      • 
          
    • Score — 1,000 down to 120 per solve, minus 250 a wrong guess and clock time.
      • 
          
    • Legend — one guess, and the line is rotated and flipped.
